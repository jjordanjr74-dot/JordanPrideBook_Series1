## 📖 MAIN STORY MANUSCRIPT (PAGES 4–27)

### **Page 4 (Spread 1 - Left)**
In the sunny heart of Pride Valley, the morning sky glowed like warm honey.  
Leo loved showing off how fast and high he could jump.  
"Watch this, Maya! I am the highest pouncer in the whole pride!" Leo cheered, taking a giant leap over the garden bushes.

### **Page 5 (Spread 1 - Right)**
Leo landed with a heavy, clumsy thump—right on top of Pop Pop’s handcrafted wooden garden marker!  
**CRACK! SNAP!**  
The smooth oak post snapped cleanly into two broken pieces beneath his blue sneakers.

### **Page 6 (Spread 2 - Left)**
Leo gasped and scrambled backward, his amber eyes wide in shock.  
Maya rushed over, clutching Glimmer tight against her yellow dress.  
"Oh no, Leo! Pop Pop’s special flower marker!" Maya whispered, her voice full of concern.

### **Page 7 (Spread 2 - Right)**
Leo’s heart pounded against his ribs like a drum.  
A heavy, uncomfortable feeling churned inside his belly—a cold, twisty tummy.  
"Maybe I can hide the pieces under these leaves," Leo panicked. "Or say a big gust of wind blew it over!"

### **Page 8 (Spread 3 - Left)**
Maya placed a gentle, comforting paw on her brother’s arm.  
"Making a mistake is scary, Leo," Maya said softly.  
"But making up a story won't make your twisty tummy go away. Lies only make fear grow."

### **Page 9 (Spread 3 - Right)**
Just then, heavy footsteps crunched on the gravel path.  
Pop Pop walked over to check on his morning seedlings, holding his wooden walking stick.  
He stopped in his tracks, looking down at his broken flower marker in the grass.

### **Page 10 (Spread 4 - Left)**
Pop Pop looked at the splintered wood, then turned to Leo and Maya.  
His amber eyes were calm and patient.  
"What happened to my special garden marker, young cubs?" Pop Pop asked quietly.

### **Page 11 (Spread 4 - Right)**
Leo felt his knees shake. The urge to make an excuse was very strong.  
*If I lie, I won't get in trouble,* a small voice whispered inside his head.  
But then Leo looked at Pop Pop’s kind face and remembered who he was.

### **Page 12 (Spread 5 - Left)**
Leo stood up straight and tall. He placed his right paw firmly over his heart.  
"It was me, Pop Pop," Leo confessed, his voice trembling slightly but staying clear.  
"I was leaping and landed right on it. I made a mistake, and I am so sorry."

### **Page 13 (Spread 5 - Right)**
Pop Pop’s face softened instantly, and a warm, proud smile spread across his lion mane.  
He knelt down on the soft grass and wrapped Leo in a giant, loving bear hug.  
"Thank you for telling me the truth, Leo. That took real lion bravery."

### **Page 14 (Spread 6 - Left)**
Pop Pop picked up the two broken pieces of carved oak and held them together.  
"A broken wooden marker can always be glued and mended," Pop Pop explained gently.  
"But family trust, once broken by a lie, is much harder to fix. Today, you chose honor over fear."

### **Page 15 (Spread 6 - Right)**
Maya cheered and jumped up and down!  
"You were so brave, Leo! Telling the truth makes you the strongest cub in the whole pride!"  
Leo felt the heavy, twisty feeling in his belly melt away, replaced by warm sunshine.

### **Page 16 (Spread 7 - Left)**
Pop Pop brought Leo and Maya inside his cozy woodworking workshop.  
"Honesty means taking responsibility," Pop Pop said. "Let's fix this together."  
Leo carefully held the tube of strong wood glue, while Pop Pop guided his paws.

### **Page 17 (Spread 7 - Right)**
Together, they set the metal clamp and let the glue dry tight.  
"Fixing a mistake takes teamwork and effort," Pop Pop smiled.  
"When we are honest in our family, we can solve any problem together!"

### **Page 18 (Spread 8 - Left)**
Mom and Dad walked into the workshop and saw the repaired garden marker.  
"What happened here?" Dad asked, wiping his mechanic hands with a rag.  
"I accidentally broke it, Dad," Leo said. "But I told Pop Pop right away, and we glued it back together!"

### **Page 19 (Spread 8 - Right)**
Dad placed a strong, proud paw on Leo’s shoulder.  
"In our family, honesty is our foundation," Dad said. "When you tell the truth, we can always help you. You showed great integrity today, son."

### **Page 20 (Spread 9 - Left)**
Once the glue was completely dry, Leo proudly carried the repaired marker back out to the garden.  
He pushed it deep into the rich, dark soil right beside Pop Pop’s blooming yellow sunflowers.  
The oak marker stood strong and straight once again.

### **Page 21 (Spread 9 - Right)**
Pop Pop gathered Leo and Maya under the shady branches of the grand acacia tree.  
The afternoon sun painted the savanna sky in beautiful shades of orange and gold.  
"Always remember our family way," Pop Pop said in his deep, gentle voice.

### **Page 22 (Spread 10 - Left)**
Pop Pop looked into their eyes with love and conviction.  
"When you tell a lie, fear controls you. But when you speak the truth, love and trust protect you."

### **Page 23 (Spread 10 - Right)**
Leo and Maya stood up together, placed their paws firmly over their hearts, and recited the Jordan Pride Creed:  
*"ONLY weak lions lie. The Jordan Pride is Fearless and Strong. Therefore, we are always honest and true!"*

### **Page 24 (Spread 11 - Left)**
The twisty feeling in Leo’s stomach was completely gone.  
In its place was a peaceful, warm glow. He knew that making a mistake was human, but choosing honesty made his family bond stronger than ever.

### **Page 25 (Spread 11 - Right)**
That evening at dinner, the Jordan Pride sat together around the wooden table, sharing food, laughter, and stories.  
Trust filled the home like a sweet, comforting blanket.

### **Page 26 (Spread 12 - Left)**
Whenever you make a mistake—big or small—remember Leo’s story.  
Don't let fear hide the truth under the leaves.  
Stand tall, take a deep breath, and let honesty show your true courage!

### **Page 27 (Spread 12 - Right)**
Because when you tell the truth, your family trust stays unbroken, and your heart stays bright and free!  
The Jordan Pride is always honest and true—and so are you!

* **Core Pillars:** Truth Builds Trust, Courage Over Fear, Taking Responsibility, Unconditional Family Love.

### **Page 29: Discussion Corner (Parent-Child Reflection)**
1. **The 'Twisty Tummy' Feeling:** Have you ever felt a 'twisty tummy' after breaking something or making a mistake? What is that feeling trying to tell you?
2. **Bravery in Truth:** Why did it take MORE bravery for Leo to tell Pop Pop the truth than to make up an excuse about the wind?
3. **Mending vs. Hiding:** How did Pop Pop help Leo fix the wooden marker after Leo told the truth? What happens when we try to hide mistakes?
4. **Family Trust:** Why is telling the truth so important inside a family? How does honesty make your family stronger?

### **Page 30: Honor Code Checklist**
* [ ] When I make an accidental mess or break something, I tell an adult right away.
* [ ] I place my **paw over my heart**, take a deep breath, and speak the truth.
* [ ] I choose honesty even when I am afraid of getting in trouble.
* [ ] I help fix my mistakes by offering to clean up, repair, or apologize.
* [ ] I remember that my family loves me and that honesty keeps our trust unbroken!
