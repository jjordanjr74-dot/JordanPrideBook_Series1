## 🌟 CORE VALUES & WORLD DESIGN

The Jordan Pride is a lion family living in a **100% anthropomorphic universe** where all animals are fashionably clothed, bipedal, and use 4-digit furry animal paws. The Jordans are a close-knit family whose values guide their every action and decision.

### 📜 The Jordan Family Core Values System
* **Honesty & Integrity**: Always telling the truth and doing what is right even when nobody is watching.
* **Loyalty & Family Support**: Family is a safe harbor where everyone pulls together.
* **Empathy & Kindness**: Warmth and understanding across cultures and differences.
* **Community Service & Helping Others**: Actively assisting neighbors and caring for nature.
* **Protecting the Weak & Strong Helping the Weak**: Using your strength and advantages to lift up others.
* **Responsibility & Accountability**: Taking ownership of actions and learning from mistakes.
* **Politeness & Good Manners**: Saying *"Yes ma'am / No ma'am"* and *"Yes sir / No sir"*, using courteous speech, and showing respect to adults and elders.
* **Love for Learning, Adventure & Nature**: Maintaining curiosity, positive attitude, and deep respect for all living things.

> **Goal of the Series**: To teach and entertain without being preachy. Readers must be engaged and excited for each new adventure.

## 🔒 ANATOMY & PRE-PRESS COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
1. **100% Anthropomorphic**: All characters are bipedal, fully clothed, and have 4-digit furry paws.
2. **Zero Human Anatomy**: No 5-fingered human hands, no human skin, no human knuckles.
3. **Zero Unrequested Text**: No speech bubbles, floating letters, or comic book sound effects in generated artwork.
4. **Maya Bow Lock**: ONLY Maya wears the purple ribbon ear bow behind her left ear. Leo has a simple hair tuft and NO bows.
